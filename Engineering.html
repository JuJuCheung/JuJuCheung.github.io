What is Engineering?

Engineering is the use of science, maths, technology and creativity to solve real-world problems. Engineers design, build, test and improve products, machines, structures and systems. Engineering can be found everywhere around us, including cars, bridges, buildings, computers, aircraft, phones, robots and medical equipment.

Main Areas of Engineering

⚙️ Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical engineering focuses on machines, engines, gears, mechanisms, moving parts and mechanical systems.

🏗️ Civil Engineering

Civil engineers design and build structures such as bridges, roads, tunnels, railways and buildings.

⚡ Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineers work with electricity, circuits, power, electronics and electrical systems.

💻 Computer Engineering

Computer engineering combines computing and electronics to develop hardware, processors and digital systems.

✈️ Aerospace Engineering

Aerospace engineers design aircraft, spacecraft, rockets, satellites and flight systems.

🤖 Robotics Engineering

Robotics combines mechanics, electronics and computing to create machines that can perform tasks.

🧪 Chemical Engineering

Chemical engineers use chemistry, physics and engineering to develop materials and industrial processes.

🌱 Environmental Engineering

Environmental engineers develop solutions for pollution, water, waste, energy and sustainability.

Engineering Design Process

1. Identify the Problem

Understand what problem needs to be solved and what the design needs to achieve.

2. Research

Find information about the problem, users, materials and existing products.

3. Generate Ideas

Develop different possible solutions through sketches, diagrams and models.

4. Choose a Solution

Compare ideas and select a suitable solution based on performance, safety and cost.

5. Design

Develop the idea using drawings, measurements, CAD or models.

6. Build

Make a prototype or working version of the proposed solution.

7. Test

Test the product to see whether it performs correctly and safely.

8. Improve

Use testing results to make changes and improve the final design.

Engineering Thinking

🧠 Problem Solving

Engineers break large problems into smaller problems and investigate possible solutions.

📐 Maths & Science

Maths and science help engineers calculate, predict and understand how designs will work.

🧪 Testing

Engineers test prototypes to identify problems before developing a final product.

💡 Innovation

Engineers develop new ideas and improve existing technology to solve problems more effectively.

“Engineering is about using knowledge and creativity to turn ideas into real solutions.”
