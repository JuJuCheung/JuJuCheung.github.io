English Language

English Language develops close reading, evaluation and precise writing. Strong responses identify methods, explain their effects and organise ideas carefully for audience, purpose and form.

Key Topics

🔍

Reading Meaning

Inference, evidence, explicit information and implicit meaning.

🧠

Language Analysis

Vocabulary, imagery, syntax, tone, semantic fields and effects.

🧱

Structure

Focus, shifts, pacing, contrast, openings and endings.

⚖️

Comparison & Evaluation

Compare viewpoints and judge how effectively writers achieve their purposes.

📝

Creative Writing

Description, narrative voice, detail, structure and technical control.

📣

Transactional Writing

Articles, speeches, letters and arguments shaped for audience and purpose.

Revision Guide

Evidence

Select short quotations that directly support your point.

Analysis

Explain how specific choices create meaning rather than only naming techniques.

Structure

Track how attention, tension and focus change across the whole text.

Comparison

Compare viewpoint and method, not just subject matter.

Writing

Plan the overall shape before writing individual sentences.

Accuracy

Control sentences, paragraphs, punctuation and spelling deliberately.

Exam Skills

🔬

Method Analysis

Identify a method only when it helps explain how the language works.

🧭

Whole-Text Structure

Comment on sequencing, focus, shifts and transitions across the extract.

🎯

Audience & Purpose

Choose register, organisation and rhetoric that suit the task.
