Physics

Physics builds models of matter, energy, motion and fields. Strong Physics answers combine equations, units, diagrams, experimental evidence and clear physical reasoning.

Key Topics

⚡

Energy & Electricity

Energy stores, energy transfer, power, circuits, resistance and electrical safety.

🌡️

Particle Model

Density, internal energy, changes of state and gas pressure.

☢️

Atomic Physics

Atomic structure, radiation, half-life, fission and fusion.

🚗

Forces

Motion, acceleration, Newton's laws, momentum and pressure.

🌊

Waves

Wave properties, electromagnetic waves, reflection, refraction and sound.

🧲

Magnetism & Space

Magnetic fields, motors, generators, transformers and astrophysics.

Revision Guide

Equations

Learn the meaning of each quantity and its unit, then practise rearranging equations.

Graphs

Interpret gradients and areas physically, not just mathematically.

Forces

Draw free-body diagrams and identify resultant forces clearly.

Waves

Keep wave speed, frequency, wavelength and amplitude separate.

Electricity

Distinguish series and parallel rules and link current, voltage, resistance and power.

Experiments

Know methods, measurements, uncertainty, control variables and improvements.

Exam Skills

📐

Mathematical Physics

📈

Graphs

🧪

Practical Reasoning

Explain measurement choices, uncertainty and control variables.
