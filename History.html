About World Revolutions

Revolutions are periods of major political or social change. Some overthrow monarchies, some seek independence, and others transform political and economic systems. They are not always peaceful, and historians often disagree about their causes and consequences.

This is a selection of major historical revolutions and revolutionary movements. Several involved more than one country.
