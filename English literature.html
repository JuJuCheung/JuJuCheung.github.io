English Literature

English Literature is about interpretation supported by precise textual evidence. Strong responses analyse language, form and structure while connecting ideas to character, theme, context and alternative readings.

Key Topics

🎭

Shakespeare

Character, themes, dramatic methods, structure and relevant context.

📖

19th-Century Novel

Narrative voice, characterisation, setting, symbolism and social context.

🎬

Modern Text

Ideas, relationships, dramatic or narrative methods and writer's purpose.

🪶

Poetry Anthology

Meaning, imagery, form, structure, sound and comparison.

🔍

Unseen Poetry

Independent close reading and comparison under timed conditions.

🧠

Critical Interpretation

Develop a thesis, analyse methods and consider plausible alternative readings.

Revision Guide

Thesis

Answer the exact question with a clear interpretation rather than a plot summary.

Evidence

Use short, well-chosen quotations and zoom in on individual words or patterns.

Methods

Discuss language, form and structure with effects tied to meaning.

Context

Integrate only context that genuinely helps explain the text.

Comparison

Compare ideas and methods throughout, rather than writing two isolated mini-essays.

Evaluation

Use tentative language for interpretations and justify them with evidence.

Exam Skills

🔎

Close Analysis

Move from quotation to method, then to connotation or pattern, then to interpretation.

🧩

Structure

Track shifts, contrasts, cyclical patterns, openings, endings and dramatic positioning.

⚖️

Alternative Readings

Present another defensible interpretation without losing your main argument.

“Strong Literature answers do not just identify techniques. They explain how the writer's choices shape meaning and interpretation.”
