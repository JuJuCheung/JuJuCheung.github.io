Geography

Geography explains how physical processes and human decisions shape places. Strong Geography answers use located examples, data, scale, systems thinking and balanced evaluation.

Key Topics

🌋

Natural Hazards

Tectonic hazards, weather hazards and climate change.

🌳

The Living World

Ecosystems, tropical rainforests and hot deserts or cold environments.

🌊

Physical Landscapes

Coasts, rivers, erosion, transport, deposition and management.

🏙️

Urban Issues

Urban growth, opportunities, challenges and sustainable cities.

💷

Changing Economic World

Development, global inequality, industry and economic change.

💧

Resources & Fieldwork

Food, water, energy, fieldwork methods, data and evaluation.

Revision Guide

Processes

Use clear sequences to explain how landforms and hazards develop.

Case Studies

Learn precise place detail, but only use evidence that is relevant to the question.

Evaluation

Compare social, economic and environmental costs and benefits over different timescales.

Maps & Data

Practise grid references, scale, graphs, statistics and identifying anomalies.

Fieldwork

Know the enquiry question, sampling, risks, data presentation, conclusions and limitations.

Synoptic Links

Connect physical and human Geography rather than treating every topic separately.

Exam Skills

🗺️

Place Evidence

Use named examples accurately and link facts directly to your argument.

📊

Data & Maps

Read scale, calculate change, interpret spatial patterns and identify anomalies.

⚖️

Evaluation

Judge strategies by stakeholder, scale, timescale and sustainability.

“Strong Geography answers explain patterns, use evidence and evaluate how different people and places are affected.”
