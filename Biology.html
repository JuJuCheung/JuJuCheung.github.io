Biology

Biology explains how living organisms are built, how they function, how they respond to change, and how organisms interact with one another and their environment.

Key Topics

🔬

Cell Biology

Cell structure, microscopy, cell division, transport across membranes and stem cells.

🫀

Organisation

Digestive system, enzymes, heart and circulation, plant tissues and disease.

🦠

Infection & Response

Pathogens, immune responses, vaccination, antibiotics and drug development.

🌱

Bioenergetics

Photosynthesis, respiration, exercise and metabolism.

🧠

Homeostasis

Nervous system, hormones, blood glucose, temperature and reproduction.

🧬

Inheritance & Ecology

DNA, genetics, evolution, ecosystems, biodiversity and human impact.

Revision Guide

Cell Processes

Link structure to function and practise microscopy, diffusion, osmosis and active transport.

Body Systems

Explain digestion, circulation and plant transport as connected systems.

Disease

Compare pathogen types and explain immune responses, vaccination and antibiotics.

Energy

Know photosynthesis and respiration equations and understand limiting factors.

Control

Use negative-feedback chains to explain nervous and hormonal control.

Inheritance & Ecology

Use genetic diagrams, natural selection and ecosystem relationships accurately.

Exam Skills

📊

Data Analysis

Quote figures, calculate change, identify patterns and explain the biology behind them.

🧪

Required Practicals

Know variables, controls, improvements, risks and how results should be processed.

✍️

Linked Explanations

Use because and therefore links so every biological step leads clearly to the next.

“Strong Biology answers do more than remember content: they use evidence, method and reasoning to explain why an answer works.”
