Key Mathematical Ideas

Show Your Working

Write down the main steps clearly. This helps you gain method marks even if the final answer is not correct.

Choose the Right Method

Look carefully at the information in the question and decide which formula, rule or method is needed.

Check Units

Make sure your units match the question, especially for length, area, volume, speed and compound measures.

Check Your Answer

Ask whether your answer is sensible. Use estimation where possible and check signs, rounding and accuracy.

Mathematical Thinking

How should I approach a Higher Maths question?

Read the question carefully. Underline the important information. Decide what you need to find. Choose a suitable method and show each main step clearly.

What makes a strong GCSE answer?

A strong answer uses the correct method, shows enough working, uses the correct units and gives the answer to the accuracy requested.

“Higher Maths is about choosing the right method, showing clear working and checking that your answer makes sense.”
