Key Mathematical Ideas

Rates and Change

Engineering uses derivatives for rates such as velocity, current and heat flow, and integrals for totals such as distance, charge and energy.

Forces and Vectors

Vectors describe quantities with both size and direction, making them essential for forces, motion and engineering geometry.

Systems and Matrices

Matrices provide a compact way to solve several linked equations at once, which is useful in structures, circuits and control systems.

Signals and Models

Differential equations and trigonometric functions help engineers model vibration, waves, electrical signals and changing systems.

Mathematical Thinking

Why is engineering maths different?

In engineering maths, mathematics is not only about getting a numerical answer. You also need to choose a suitable model, use the correct units, apply the right formula, and explain what the answer means in an engineering situation.

What makes a strong solution?

A strong solution is logically organised. It identifies the known values, chooses the correct engineering maths method, shows the main steps, keeps track of units, and checks whether the final answer is reasonable.
