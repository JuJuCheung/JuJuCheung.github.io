Key Mathematical Ideas

Limits and Change

Calculus turns the idea of “getting closer” into a precise mathematical tool. Limits lead to derivatives and integrals.

Structure and Transformation

Linear algebra studies spaces and transformations. Matrices give a compact way to represent and calculate them.

Uncertainty and Evidence

Probability measures uncertainty, while statistics uses data to estimate, compare and make carefully justified conclusions.

Patterns in Integers

Number theory asks simple-looking questions about whole numbers that can lead to deep ideas about primes and congruences.

Mathematical Thinking

Why is university maths different?

At university level, mathematics is not only about getting a numerical answer. You also need to understand definitions, recognise useful structures, choose an appropriate theorem, and explain why a method works.

What makes a strong solution?

A strong solution is logically organised. It states the important idea, shows the main steps, checks assumptions where necessary, and reaches a conclusion that follows from the mathematics.

“Mathematics is not simply calculating. It is recognising structure, making logical arguments and explaining why a result must be true.”
