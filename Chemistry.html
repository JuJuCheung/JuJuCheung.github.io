⚗️ About Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of substances, their properties, structures and chemical reactions.

In GCSE Chemistry, you will learn about atoms, bonding, chemical reactions, energy changes and how chemistry is used in everyday life.

This website covers all 10 main AQA GCSE Chemistry topics.

Each topic includes:

  • GCSE-level revision notes
  • Important scientific definitions
  • Worked examples
  • 10 multiple-choice questions
  • Answers and explanations
  • Scores and achievements

📚 GCSE Chemistry Revision Guide

These are the main areas you should understand before your GCSE exams.

⚛️

Atomic Structure

Understand protons, neutrons, electrons, isotopes and the periodic table.

Practise calculating the number of particles inside atoms.

🔗

Chemical Bonding

Learn ionic, covalent and metallic bonding.

Explain how bonding affects melting points, conductivity and physical properties.

⚖️

Chemistry Calculations

Practise relative formula mass, moles, concentration and percentage yield.

Remember to show your working.

🧫

Chemical Reactions

Revise acids, metals, neutralisation, electrolysis and displacement reactions.

🔥

Energy Changes

Understand exothermic and endothermic reactions.

Learn activation energy and reaction profile diagrams.

⏱️

Reaction Rates

Understand collision theory, catalysts and how temperature, concentration and surface area affect reaction speed.

🛢️

Organic Chemistry

Learn about crude oil, hydrocarbons, alkanes, alkenes and cracking.

🔬

Chemical Analysis

Revise chromatography, gas tests, flame tests and identifying unknown substances.

🌍

The Atmosphere

Understand greenhouse gases, climate change, air pollution and Earth's early atmosphere.

♻️

Using Resources

Learn about recycling, water treatment, corrosion, sustainability and the Haber process.

📝 GCSE Chemistry Exam Skills

🧮 Calculations

For calculation questions:

  1. Write the correct formula.
  2. Substitute the numbers.
  3. Calculate carefully.
  4. Write the correct units.

Example:

Moles = Mass ÷ Mr

36 ÷ 18 = 2 mol

💬 Scientific Explanations

Use correct scientific vocabulary in your answers.

Important words include:

  • Electrostatic attraction
  • Delocalised electrons
  • Activation energy
  • Collision frequency
  • Oxidation and reduction

🧪 Required Practicals

Revise the methods used in chemistry experiments.

Remember:

  • Independent variable
  • Dependent variable
  • Control variables
  • Safety precautions
  • Accuracy and improvements

📊 Graph Questions

When drawing a graph:

  • Label both axes.
  • Include units.
  • Use a suitable scale.
  • Plot points accurately.
  • Draw an appropriate line.

📖 Command Words

Describe: Say what happens.

Explain: Give scientific reasons.

Compare: Give similarities and differences.

Evaluate: Consider advantages, disadvantages and evidence.

🎯 Exam Technique

Read each question carefully.

  • Underline important words.
  • Check the number of marks.
  • Use scientific terminology.
  • Show calculation steps.
  • Check answers and units.
