English Literature

English Literature develops your ability to understand novels, plays and poetry. Strong answers explain how writers use language, structure, characters and themes to communicate ideas.

Key Topics

📖

Novels

Explore plot, character development, themes, setting and narrative methods.

🎭

Drama

Study dialogue, stage directions, conflict, relationships and dramatic structure.

🪶

Poetry

Analyse imagery, form, rhythm, rhyme, voice and meaning.

💡

Themes

Explore ideas such as power, conflict, love, responsibility and identity.

👤

Characters

Track how characters change and how writers present their thoughts and actions.

🏛️

Context

Understand how historical, social and cultural ideas can influence literature.

Revision Guide

Quotations

Learn short quotations that can be used for several themes and characters.

Characters

Track each major character across the beginning, middle and end of the text.

Themes

Connect themes to characters, key moments and writer's methods.

Language

Analyse individual words, imagery and literary techniques closely.

Structure

Consider beginnings, endings, contrasts, turning points and changes in focus.

Context

Use relevant context to support your analysis, not as a separate history paragraph.

Exam Skills

🔍

Close Analysis

Zoom in on important words and explain their meanings and effects.

🧠

Interpretation

Develop clear ideas about characters, themes and the writer's message.

🧩

Whole Text

Link the extract or quotation to events elsewhere in the text.

“Strong English Literature answers explain what the writer is showing, how they show it and why it is important.”
