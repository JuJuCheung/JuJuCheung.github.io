What Does A2B Do?

A2B teaches students important digital skills that can help them make safer and smarter choices when they are online.

🔒

Stay Safe Online

Learn how to protect your personal information, passwords and online accounts.

💬

Communicate Safely

Learn how to communicate respectfully and what to do if something online makes you uncomfortable.

🔎

Research Smartly

Learn how to check whether online information is reliable, accurate or misleading.

💻

Digital Responsibility

Learn how to use websites, apps, social media and technology responsibly.

Need to Talk to Someone?

If something happens online that worries, scares or upsets you, you do not have to deal with it alone.

Talk to a parent, teacher or another trusted adult. You can also use Childline to speak to a trained counsellor online.

💬 Talk to Childline

If you or somebody else is in immediate danger, call 999.
