What is A2B?

A2B is an interactive digital learning website that teaches students important skills for using technology and the internet. The internet is useful for learning, communicating, gaming and entertainment, but it is also important to understand how to protect yourself and make sensible choices online.

Why Does Online Safety Matter?

What you do online can affect both you and other people. Small decisions can make a big difference.

Remember:

  • 🔑 Never share your passwords with other people.
  • 📍 Think carefully before sharing your location or personal details.
  • 🔗 Do not open suspicious links or unexpected downloads.
  • 📸 Think before posting pictures because online posts can be difficult to remove.
  • 🤝 Treat other people respectfully online.
  • 🚩 Block and report users who send harmful or inappropriate messages.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Speak to a trusted adult when something online worries you.
💬

Need to Talk to Someone?

If something happens online that makes you feel worried, scared or uncomfortable, you do not have to deal with it alone.

You can speak to a parent, carer, teacher or another trusted adult. You can also contact Childline for support.

💬 Talk to Childline Online

If you or somebody else is in immediate danger, call 999.
